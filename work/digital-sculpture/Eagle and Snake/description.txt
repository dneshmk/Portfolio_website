A detailed 3D model inspired by the symbolism of the medical staff, reimagined with a bold artistic twist. The design features an eagle at the top, two intertwined snakes coiling around the central staff, and a traditional diya element integrated into the composition. The overall form resembles the Staff of Hermes, blending mythological and cultural symbolism into a single sculptural piece.

This model was carefully designed with 3D printing in mind, focusing on structural balance, clean geometry, and print-ready detailing. The project demonstrates my skills in concept development, digital sculpting, and preparing optimized models for physical production.
