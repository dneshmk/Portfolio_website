This documentary film was produced for Siddhartha Das Studio as a dedicated visual exploration of the traditional Dhokra craft. The film carefully documents the entire creative journey of making Dhokra idols — from the initial preparation of clay and the delicate process of wax modeling to metal casting, detailing, and the final finishing stages.

Through close observation and cinematic storytelling, the film highlights the precision, patience, and generational knowledge embedded in this ancient metal-casting technique. It not only captures the technical process but also reflects the cultural depth and artistic value that make Dhokra an enduring and significant craft tradition.

Created as an educational and archival work, the project aims to bring greater awareness to the importance of preserving indigenous art practices and supporting artisan communities.
